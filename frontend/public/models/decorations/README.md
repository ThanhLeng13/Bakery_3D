# Cake decoration assets

Original procedural assets authored for Bơ Nơ Bakery; generated by
`frontend/scripts/generate-decoration-models.js`. No downloaded meshes,
photographic textures, image-to-3D service, or external decoder is used.

Run `node scripts/generate-decoration-models.js` from `frontend/` to regenerate.
Each GLB is centred in X/Z and has its lowest point at Y=0. Its material groups
are merged to keep draw calls low. Dimensions are in the builder's local units.

- `flower.glb`: layered buttercream rose with curved petals and two leaves.
- `strawberry.glb`: smooth red skin with shallow seed dimples, seeds, calyx and stem;
  gently tilted to rest on the icing.
- `macaron.glb`: rounded shells, cream filling and continuous ruffled feet.
- `candle.glb`: retained asset; not a selectable option until its product/price is defined.

These are stylized, editable product illustrations, not scans of actual bakery products.
Keep the procedural fallbacks in Cake3D for loading failures.
